The Two Weekends

Jazz Fest Schedule

Weekend 1

The Opening

Last weekend of April. Typically features huge headliners and the classic opening ceremonies.

  • Thursday - Sunday
  • Fair Grounds Race Course
  • Gates open at 11am daily
  • Music ends at 7pm
Weekend 2

The Finale

First weekend of May. Often has the biggest names and the most epic closing sets.

  • Thursday - Sunday
